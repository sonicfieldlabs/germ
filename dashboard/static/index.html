	                        About germ
	                        
A modular lab for generative microsound

	                        
germ treats sound as living matter: something generated, granulated, mutated, routed, controlled, and traced through lineage.

	                      

	                      
	                    

	                    

	                      

	                        Deep simple idea
	                        
Take any sound, extract what matters from it, use that as modulation, grow new sounds from it, keep the best mutations, and build families of related material.

	                      

	                      

	                        

	                          01
	                          Sound matter
	                          
Waveforms, regions, masks, loops, candidates, sources, and cultures.

	                        

	                        

	                          02
	                          Micro layer
	                          
Grains, cells, quanta, swarms, membranes, colonies, and spectral tissue.

	                        

	                        

	                          03
	                          Generation layer
	                          
Generate, mutate, continue, inpaint, graft, batch, LoRA strains, and model routing.

	                        

	                        

	                          04
	                          Control + genetics
	                          
Audio-to-control, CV/MIDI/OSC, identity extraction, candidate ecology, and culture maps.

	                        

	                      

	                      

	                        Every generated sound can become
	                        

	                          a source
	                          a prompt
	                          a modulator
	                          a mask
	                          a rhythm
	                          a material identity
	                          a parent
	                          a candidate
	                          a reusable memory
	                        

	                      

	                      

	                        
GERM spans micro-time grains, meso-scale colonies, and macro sound organisms in one graph.
